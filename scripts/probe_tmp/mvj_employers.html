Overview:
Visa sponsors are U.S.-based employers who have a track record of hiring and sponsoring foreign workers for employment-based visas. The website provides data and tools to connect job seekers with these employers and understand their sponsorship trends.

Sponsorship:
Visa sponsors must comply with immigration laws and demonstrate a need for hiring foreign workers. They often sponsor employees for work visas like H-1B, L-1, O-1, or PERM-based green cards.

  • Employer Advanced Search
  • Simple Search
Employer Roles:
Employers file petitions, such as Form I-129 for H-1B or similar forms, to request approval for the foreign worker to legally work in the U.S. They may also provide support for green card applications via the PERM Labor Certification process.

Data Tools:
Myvisajobs provides comprehensive lists of visa sponsors, including details such as number of labor petitions filed, job titles and salaries for sponsored positions, work locations, and approval and denial rates for petitions.

For Job Seekers:
MyVisaJobs helps international job seekers identify potential employers who are experienced in sponsoring work visas. Features like labor petitions filing, employer profiles, and H-1B Visa and Green Card Annual Reports assist candidates in making informed decisions.
