Our application process
  • You’ll get an instant application receipt, via email, as soon as you have applied online.
  • Once we’ve reviewed your application and found a suitable role match, we’ll set up an HR introduction phone call.
  • Suitable applicants will then be invited to an interview with the hiring manager.
  • An assessment, in the form of a second interview with a practical exercise, may be set up – with all details clearly shared in advance.
  • Final check with reference checks and cognitive test
  • If successful we’ll share an offer and once accepted arrange a full onboarding process.
Our CODES values
At Trinasolar, our core values are the foundation of everything we do!
 They shape our culture, guide our actions, and inspire us to improve continuously. From putting customers first and embracing innovation to dedication, excellence, and teamwork, these values reflect our integrity and drive. Here’s how we live by our C·O·D·E·S:
C
Focus on the customer
Our commitment to "focus on the customer" drives us to deeply understand customer needs, respond quickly, and offer empowering solutions. Customer satisfaction fuels our success and inspires us to deliver outstanding service every day!
O
Persist in open innovation
"Open innovation" means staying open-minded, tackling challenges head-on, and valuing diverse perspectives. We foster a collaborative space where ideas flourish and innovation thrives, propelling us forward with agility and resilience. 
D
Dedication & hard work
“Dedication” is our foundation. We persevere with a grounded approach, embracing responsibility and positivity. With focus, persistence, and a commitment to excellence, we’re ready to overcome any challenge together.
E
Strive for excellence
Our "strive for excellence" core value encourages us to exceed limits, set high standards, and pursue lifelong learning. We’re dedicated to achieving ambitious goals and delivering outstanding results through constant growth and improvement.
S
Share responsibility & create value together
We believe in accountability and teamwork. "Share responsibility" reflects our commitment to working together toward shared goals. We celebrate collective successes and recognise the achievements of each team member, creating value and unity.
