02Who We're Looking For

The Ideal MIXUE Franchisee

MIXUE is not the right brand for every operator. We look for partners who personally lead a long-term business, not a passive investment.

Operator, not Investor

An operator, not an investor.

Commit full-time attention and maximum effort to operating the business, including personal involvement in restaurant management, financial responsibility for reinvestment, and hands-on business leadership.

(Minimum of 60 hours in-store per month, including at least 3 full working days on-site.)

Financial Minimum

USD $400,000

Minimum liquid assets and net worth requirements.

Real Estate

The ability to source the right real estate.

Demonstrated ability to identify and develop high-traffic retail locations.

Long Term Orientation

Long term orientation.

Strong alignment with MIXUE's values, culture, and long-term operating philosophy.
