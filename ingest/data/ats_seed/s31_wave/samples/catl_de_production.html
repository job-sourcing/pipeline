Our production department

In our cell and module production facilities, battery cells as well as modules for various automotive customers are produced on different production lines. The first step is the cell production, which partly has to take place under clean room conditions. This makes battery cell production even more complex than the production of a modern chip factory.

The individual cells are then processed into modules in module production and subsequently delivered to customers. Both production areas are highly automated and characterized by robots and driverless transport systems.

If you want to learn more about the daily work in our two production areas, click on the pictures of Jonny and Maria.
