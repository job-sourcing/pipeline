Working @ Sirnaomics

Come explore the limitless potential of RNA-targeted therapy with us.

At Sirnaomics, we’re not just offering jobs — we’re building a bold, global playground for innovators. Powered by our world-class RNA-targeted technology platform, we’re a tight-knit team of science fanatics driven to make real impact. Your skills won’t just be used — they’ll lead the charge on breakthrough pipelines, turning bold ideas into life-changing therapies for patients who need them most.

Diversity powers our edge. We thrive on different perspectives, fueling sharper science and faster progress. This is your chance to create, not just execute — work alongside top researchers, shape the future of RNA medicine, and own your impact.

We’ve got your back with competitive pay, great benefits, and flexible work that fits real life.

Join the revolution. Let’s redefine RNA-targeted therapy — together.
