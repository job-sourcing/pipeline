About Us

About Us

SphereEx

We specialize in building distributed data infrastructures and delivering a SaaS experience through the cloud. Our company was founded by the core team of Apache ShardingSphere, a well-known open-source ecosystem that provides a platform-based, pluggable, and cloud-native solution to transform any database into a distributed database system.

With features like data sharding, elastic scaling, encryption, and more, ShardingSphere is the ideal solution for modern enterprises looking to tackle complex data application scenarios created by increasing database fragmentation.

At SphereEx, we're committed to empowering businesses to easily manage their data through pioneering concepts like "Database Plus" and "Database Mesh." These concepts are designed to unlock innovative data usage and enable businesses to thrive in today's data-driven world. With our expertise in distributed data infrastructures and the power of ShardingSphere, we offer businesses a comprehensive solution to their data management needs
