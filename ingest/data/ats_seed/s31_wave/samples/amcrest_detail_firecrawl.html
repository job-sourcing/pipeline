NetSuite Developer

The NetSuite Developer will design and implement configurations, customizations, and integrations for supporting the continued growth of our business processes. An ideal candidate will need to be self-motivated, capable of working with limited direction, and adaptable enough to adjust to changing priorities.

Essential duties and responsibilities include, but are not limited to:

  • Design, develop, test, deploy, and maintain custom solutions
  • Integrating third party applications as needed
  • Be the primary point of contact and manage all NetSuite support cases
  • Support company analysis by building searches, reports, KPIs, and dashboards
  • Manage roles and permissions
  • Maintain custom forms
  • Collaborate with users to support their optimized use of NetSuite
  • Experience with designing and developing web based solutions
  • Ability to troubleshoot major/minor technical incidents
  • Ability to work on multiple projects simultaneously, keep momentum on each and manage project due dates

Preferred skills, education, and experience are:

  • Bachelor’s degree in relevant discipline is preferred
  • Minimum of 1-2 years NetSuite experience
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, organizational, interpersonal, and communicative skills
  • Proficiency writing object-oriented JavaScript and PL/SQL
  • Ability to automate business / ERP processes
  • Direct experience with NetSuite preferred including practical knowledge of SuiteFlow, SuiteScript, and SuiteAnalytics
  • Prior financial/accounting experience specifically related to auditing GL Impact as well as P&L reports in NetSuite.
  • Prior experience implementing company processes and workflows to NetSuite, or migrating to NetSuite from another ERP.
  • Meticulous attention to detail
