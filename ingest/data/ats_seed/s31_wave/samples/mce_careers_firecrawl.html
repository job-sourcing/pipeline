About MedChemExpress LLC

Headquartered in the United States, MedChemExpress LLC ("MCE") operates branch offices and warehouses in Europe and China. Since 2008, we have supplied products to many renowned research institutes, laboratories, biotechnology companies, and pharmaceutical organizations worldwide. MCE collaborates with more than 500 partners globally to discover innovative and effective ways to advance life sciences and improve human health.

MCE's offers a broad range of high-quality research chemicals and biochemicals, including novel life science reagents, reference compounds, APIs, and natural compounds for laboratory and scientific research. We take pride in delivering products of the highest quality. Product identity, purity, quality, and activity are ensured through our rigorous quality control programs and procedures. To support our customers, we provide analytical data such as HNMR, LC-MS, HPLC, stability testing, and activity assays for our products.

Sales Representative/Manager

Full Job Description

This is a full-time position with career advancement opportunities within the company. You must be available to work from Monday to Friday and willing to travel.
MedChemExpress (MCE) is a renowned globally company in the chemicals and biochemicals research market specializing in the development of novel life-science reagents, reference compounds, APIs and natural compounds, and MedChemExpress (MCE) is rapidly expanding its sales team to further develop more customers.

Location based

Belgium, France, Germany, India, Italy, Japan, Netherlands, Spain, UK, Hong Kong, Switzerland

Your role

1. Ability to work independently to achieve sales growth and reach sales target for the MedChemExpress (MCE) product line in your sales area.
2. Promote MedChemExpress (MCE) brand and products in the responsible sales area.
3. Ability to interact well with customers and maintain good customer relationships, visit customers regularly, develop new customers, being professional and courteous.
4. Ability to professionally receive customer calls, chats and emails regarding orders and technical questions.
5. Ability to communicate accurate information regarding products, prices, product availability, and current promotions to customers.
6. Ability to work as an effective team player to ensure the highest level of efficiency and success for the team.
7. Cooperate with BD manager and marketing manager to participate in product branding and marketing events.
8. Ability to collect local market and industry information, do market research and share with the team to help with marketing strategies and plans.
9. Excellent business communications skills (verbal, written, presentation), organizational skills and computer skills.
10. Capacity to monitor and engage in relevant social discussions about our company, competitors, and/or industry, both from existing customers and leads for new audiences.
11. Accept customer inquiries and build customer files; follow up order processing and delivery; provide after-sale service.
12. Attend exhibitions, scientific conference, trade shows; develop marketing campaign; collect customer information.

Required Skills

1. Bachelor's degree or above in life science, Bioscience, Biology, Pharmacy, chemistry, drug chemistry, biochemistry and related disciplines.
2. Familiarity with biological or biomedical or biochemical or pharmaceutical industry, at least one year of relevant working experience (Preferred).
3. Willing to engage in sales work; can withstand certain work pressure.
4. Friendly and talkative, open-minded, strong sense of responsibility.
5. Good communication skills, ambition, strong customer service awareness.
6. Fluent in the local language and English, both oral and written.
7. High level of self-motivation and perseverance.

What you will get in return

1. Full support from local based MedChemExpress (MCE) team.
2. Onboarding training and weekly sales training program offered.
3. Basic salary plus sales commission and new customer development rewards; Uncapped earning potential.
