Senior / Advanced Medical Blood Monitoring Sensor Engineer (Algorithm Focus)
Job Responsibilities:

You will collaborate with the team to take ownership of the following responsibilities:


1.Product Development: Participate in the full lifecycle development of blood monitoring sensor products, from proof of concept to mass production. 

2.Cross-functional Collaboration & Algorithm Development: Develop biomedical signal processing algorithms, including filtering, noise reduction, feature extraction, calibration algorithms, and AI models for prediction and correction. Work with embedded systems engineers and develop a working understanding of hardware design and embedded software integration.

3.Technical Problem Solving: Understand sensing principles such as electrochemical methods and photoplethysmography (PPG), and address challenges related to signal interference, long-term stability, and measurement accuracy. 

4.Clinical & Regulatory Support: Support clinical data collection and analysis, ensuring compliance with medical device standards and regulations (e.g., ISO 13485, FDA, NMPA). 

Qualifications:

Minimum Requirements:


1.Master’s degree or above in Biomedical Engineering, Microelectronics, Automation, Computer Science, Clinical Medicine, or related fields. 

2.Strong interest in blood component analysis or vital sign monitoring, with relevant project experience (e.g., glucose monitoring devices, blood gas analyzers, wearable heart rate or SpO₂ devices preferred). 

3.Strong interdisciplinary communication skills, with the ability to understand at least one adjacent technical domain beyond one’s core expertise (e.g., algorithm engineers who can read circuit schematics or understand embedded software concepts). 


Preferred Qualifications (Plus Points)


1.Regulatory & Standards Knowledge: Familiarity with medical device software development processes (IEC 62304) and regulatory requirements for algorithm traceability and validation under NMPA/FDA frameworks. 

2.Publications & Patents: Publications in peer-reviewed journals or patents in sensor algorithms or related fields.

2.Familiar with GMP equipment management requirements, with solid understanding of equipment receipt, IQ/OQ/PQ, maintenance, calibration, validation, and lifecycle management. 

3.Proficient in Microsoft Word and Excel, capable of independently preparing SOPs, technical documents, and asset reports. 

4.Valid electrician certificate is a plus.

Regulatory Affairs Specialist
Job Responsibilities:

1.Independently responsible for regulatory submissions of products in target markets including the United States, European Union, Japan, and Canada. Prepare regulatory documentation, complete product registrations, amendments, and clinical trial approvals, ensuring high-quality deliverables and successful approvals. Activities include but are not limited to: regulatory pathway planning, compilation of regulatory and standards lists, registration testing, preparation of submission dossiers, response to deficiency questions (e.g., RFIs), and change impact assessments；

2.Guide subsidiary companies in EU regulatory activities, including: 

1）Developing regulatory strategies based on product planning, scheduling submission timelines, and overseeing the entire registration process；

2）Reviewing regulatory submission materials, participating in key technical reviews, and guiding responses to regulatory questions and deficiencies. 

3.Stay updated on global regulatory developments and policy trends.
