XCMG Profile

Since 1943, XCMG has been a trailblazer and leader in China's construction machinery industry, is now illuminating the industry worldwide with the strength and innovation of our products, embodying the spirit of China's manufacturing excellence.
Our diverse business spans mining, agriculture, sanitation machinery, emergency rescue equipment, and commercial vehicles. With 80 years of expertise, XCMG products now reach over 190 countries and regions, covering more than 95% of the Belt and Road markets. By integrating cutting-edge technology with unparalleled service through more than 2,000 global service centers, we contribute to building a shared world with the strength of a manufacturing powerhouse.
About Us
Sustainability Matters

XCMG is deeply committed to sustainability and social responsibility, which are integral to our identity and actions. We focus on building a better world through our "XCMG Making the World Better" initiative, covering disaster relief, education, poverty alleviation, industry development, and environmental protection.

With 14 global projects such as "African Water Cellars", and the "Global Operator Program", we have established a unique and targeted path for international charity. Over the past decade, XCMG has contributed over 200 million RMB in donations and resources, including 235,800 items for disaster relief. Our efforts have earned us prestigious honors like the "China Charity Award" and recognition as a "National Earthquake Relief Hero Collective". We remain dedicated to creating a sustainable and shared future.
