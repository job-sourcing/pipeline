About Us

We innovate for patients.

HUTCHMED aims to become a major pharmaceutical company developing innovative drugs for both the Chinese and global markets.

We invite motivated individuals to join our team.

In return for your talent and enthusiasm, we are committed to

providing a challenging and collaborative work environment that is

both rewarding and fulfilling.

![图片](https://public-cdn.mokahr.com/hutchmed/81f78735-0437-4789-a4b1-ca833e3bc57e.png)

**PURPOSE**

Our Group's purpose is to improve the lives of patients globally through the discovery, development and delivery of world class treatments for cancer and immunological diseases, underpinned by the business values of innovation, collaboration, integrity and sustainability across all levels of the Group.

**MISSION**

To discover, develop and bring innovative medicines to patients

worldwide

**VISION**

To be a leading innovative biopharmaceutical company to improve lives globally, driven by medical need

**VALUES**

Innovative   ●  Pragmatic   ●  Collaborative   ●  Efficient
