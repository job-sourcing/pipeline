With a strong focus on innovation and international collaboration, BIOJAPAN provides an ideal platform for connecting global partners and advancing new opportunities in drug discovery and development.

Venue: Pacifico Yokohama, Japan

AI-Driven + Organoid + Integrated Dry-Wet NAMs Platform

Medicilon continues to advance its AI-driven + organoid + integrated dry-wet NAMs (New Approach Methodologies) platform, integrating emerging technologies to support more efficient and innovative approaches to preclinical drug development.

The platform encompasses AI-enabled drug discovery, organoid and organ-on-chip technologies, PBPK modeling, and in vitro toxicology, providing diverse strategies for evaluating drug candidates and supporting translational research.

From Target to IND

As a full-service preclinical CRO, Medicilon provides integrated support spanning drug discovery, pharmaceutical research, preclinical research, and IND submission support, helping innovative drug developers advance programs efficiently through critical stages of development.

With extensive experience supporting global drug development programs, Medicilon has contributed to more than 690 IND approvals across projects submitted to regulatory authorities in China and overseas.

In addition, Medicilon has supported more than 20 overseas licensing deals for innovative drugs, further demonstrating its experience in supporting global drug development and international collaboration.

With capabilities covering multiple drug modalities and a broad range of preclinical services, Medicilon is committed to supporting partners throughout their drug development journey—from Target to IND.
